# Arranging the collage

## Layouts

Choose the layout in the **Style** step.

- **Mosaic** (the default): photos fill columns of different heights, keeping close to their own
  shapes. You can set the number of **columns**, or leave it on **Auto**, which picks a count
  that suits the photos and the size.
- **Gallery**: the collage is cut into panels, one per photo, edge to edge. It suits collages
  where every photo should get a clear frame. Columns do not apply.

Either way, the first arrangement shown is the best of many tries: the one that crops the photos
least. The Photos step's status line shows how much is trimmed in total.

## Shuffling

- **Shuffle layout** makes a new arrangement. With several pages it is **Shuffle layout within
  pages**: every page is arranged again, and each photo stays on its page.
- **Shuffle all between pages** (with several pages) mixes the photos across pages, keeping how
  many each page has.

If you have swapped or moved photos by hand, shuffling asks first, since it would undo that.

## The seed

Every arrangement comes from a number, its **seed**, shown in the address and in the **Technical
configuration** section of the Style step. Typing a seed there (or opening a link with
`?seed=...`) gives the same arrangement again for the same photos and settings. Back and forward
in the browser step through the arrangements you have seen.

## Pages

A collage is a book of **pages**, one by default. Each page is its own image of the output size,
arranged on its own.

- **Add page**: the **+ Add page** button beside the last page adds a blank page after it (up to
  20 pages). The **Pages** count in the Poster step sets the number of pages and shares the photos
  evenly across them, in order.
- **The page pill** above each page (from two pages on) shows its number and photo count, and has
  **Move left**, **Move right**, **Save this page** and **Remove page**.
- **Removing a page** asks first; its photos move to the page before it (the next one for the
  first page). Back at one page, the collage is a plain collage again.
- **Moving photos between pages:** in the Photos step, drag photos onto another page's group (on a
  touch screen, press and hold, then drag), or drop them on the **new page** target to make a page
  for them. With photos selected, **Page** in the selected-photo panel moves them to another page.
  Swapping two photos on different pages also swaps their pages.

An empty page is kept (also over a refresh) and skipped when saving.
