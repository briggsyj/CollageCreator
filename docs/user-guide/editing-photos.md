# Editing photos

## The tools

The tools sit at the top right (along the top on a phone):

- **Pan** (the default): drag to move around the collage. Hold **Space** with any tool to pan
  for as long as it is held.
- **Swap**: drag one photo onto another to swap them (or click one, then the other). Works across
  pages.
- **Select**: click a photo to select it. Shift-click adds photos to the selection or takes them
  out. Clicking beside the photos, or choosing another tool, lets go of the selection.

You can also select photos in the **Photos** step's list (Shift-click there too).

## Photo actions

While photos are selected, the **photo actions** appear beside the tools. They are coloured in the
accent colour and filled while on, which sets them apart from the tools.

- **Wider** gives the photo two columns (Mosaic) or more width (Gallery). Press again to go back.
- **Taller** gives the photo more height, in steps: 2 rows, 3 rows, then back to normal. The
  button shows **×2** or **×3** while it applies.
- **Crop** chooses which part of the photo shows (below).

A size request is kept through new arrangements, size changes and added photos, and the Photos
list shows it on the photo's thumbnail ("wider · taller ×2"). With several photos selected, Wider
and Taller apply to all of them.

## Cropping

Each photo fills its cell and is trimmed to fit. Crop chooses what is trimmed.

1. Select a photo and press **Crop** (or double-click the photo with the Select tool).
2. Drag the photo to move it inside its cell. Use the slider under Crop to zoom into it (up to
   500%). The arrow keys nudge it.
3. Press the round **✓** button, **Enter**, or Crop again to finish. Clicking beside the photos
   or on another photo also finishes; the crop is kept.

To undo a crop, zoom the slider all the way out. Holding **Space** while cropping pans the view,
and cropping carries on when you let go. Crop works on one photo at a time; with several selected
it is turned off.

## Removing

**Remove selected photo** (or **Remove n photos**) is in the Photos step's selected-photo panel,
and **Delete** works on the preview. It asks first; **Undo remove** brings the photos back.

## Moving to another page

With several pages, the selected-photo panel has **Page**, which moves the selected photos to
another page (they go last there). See [Arranging](arranging.md#pages) for dragging.
