# Getting started

A collage in four steps.

1. **Add photos.** Press **Add images...** in the Photos step, or drag photos (or a zipped
   folder of them) anywhere onto the page. A collage appears straight away: the first
   arrangement shown is the best of many tries.
2. **Pick a size.** In the **Poster** step choose a size such as **Screen**, **A4** or
   **Instagram post**, and landscape or portrait.
3. **Make it yours.** In the **Style** step choose **Mosaic** or **Gallery**, set the border
   and corners, or press **Shuffle layout** for a new arrangement. Select a photo with the
   **Select** tool to make it **Wider**, **Taller** or to **Crop** it. Use **Swap** to drag one
   photo onto another.
4. **Save.** Choose PNG, JPEG or PDF and press **Save**. A collage with several pages, or an
   Instagram post, comes as a zip of images (or, for pages, one PDF).

Your work is kept in this browser, so closing the tab or refreshing does not lose it (see
[Privacy and saved work](privacy-and-storage.md)).

## Tips

- **Share an arrangement:** the address bar holds the arrangement's seed (`?seed=...`). With
  the same photos and settings, the same seed gives the same collage. The browser's back and
  forward buttons step through earlier arrangements.
- **Zoom in to check details:** scroll over the collage, pinch on a touch screen, or use **+**
  and **−**. **Fit** shows it all again.
- **Undo a removal:** **Undo remove** (or Ctrl+Z / ⌘Z on the preview) brings back the photos
  you removed last.
