# Adding photos

## Ways to add photos

- Press **Add images...** in the Photos step and choose files.
- Drag files from your computer onto the page; anywhere works.
- Drop a **.zip** file (for example a zipped folder): the images inside are added, folders and
  hidden or system files (such as `.DS_Store` and `__MACOSX`) are skipped, and any other files
  are named in a notice.
- Before any photo is added, the empty collage is a drop area with the same button.

Photos added later go on the **last page** of a collage with several pages.

## Supported formats

JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF (first frame), BMP, and **HEIC/HEIF** from iPhones. Browsers that
cannot open HEIC themselves use a decoder bundled with the app, loaded the first time one is
needed; it still runs on your device.

Photos taken sideways are turned the right way using the orientation the camera recorded.

## Duplicates and problems

A file that is already in the collage (same name, size and date) is not added twice. Files that
could not be added are summarised in a notice under the button, with **Details** listing them
by reason: already added, not an image, or could not be read.

## How many photos

There is no fixed limit. Collages of 100 photos and more work; the preview uses small copies of
each photo, and saving decodes the full photos a few at a time so the tab stays responsive.

## Removing photos

- Select photos (see [Editing photos](editing-photos.md)) and press **Remove selected photo**
  (or **Remove n photos**) in the Photos step, or press **Delete** on the preview.
- **Remove all** takes every photo out and goes back to one page.

Both ask first. **Undo remove** brings back what was removed last, including the pages that
Remove all took away.
